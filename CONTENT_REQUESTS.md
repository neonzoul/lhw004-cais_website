# CAIS content request list

Keep this request light: the school can send whatever already exists; the project team can organise and edit it.

## Needed before publication

1. Approved Vision, Mission and one-sentence school description.
2. Official curriculum name/wording, age-to-class map, hours and current availability language.
3. Management Team names, exact role titles, short biographies and approved portraits.
4. One current event or announcement: title, date, time, location, short description, registration/contact action and one approved image.
5. Outdoor/community partners the school wants to name, including activity purpose, safeguarding context and approved evidence/images.
6. Admissions contact owner, response expectation, visit scheduling process, required form fields and privacy/consent wording.
7. Approved media folder and confirmation that each child/staff image may be published on the website.
8. First Article owner and one real school practice/activity that can be explained with evidence.

## Helpful, not blocking the next review

- Brand guide or preferred colours/fonts.
- School calendar highlights for the next 60–90 days.
- Testimonials only where the speaker and publication permission are documented.
- Policies, accreditation or awards only with current source documents.
