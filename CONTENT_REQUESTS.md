# CAIS v0.3 — lightweight confirmation list

Keep this request light: the school can send whatever already exists; the project team can organise and edit it.

## Needed before publication

1. Confirm the official Thai/English school name, phone, email, full postal address and exact Google Maps pin. Current public sources do not fully agree on the address.
2. Confirm the British curriculum wording, class names, age/cutoff mapping, school hours, term dates and availability language.
3. Confirm the Management Team names, exact role titles, short biographies and approved portraits.
4. Confirm how a parent should enquire: owner, expected response time, visit/online-meeting options, required fields and privacy/consent wording.
5. Confirm the fee information and admissions documents that may be published or requested privately.
6. Confirm that each supplied child/staff photo and video may be published on the website. A simple approved/not-approved list is enough.
7. Ask one CAIS educator to review the Curriculum page and first article for factual accuracy.
8. Share one current event, classroom practice or school-life story when convenient; the team can organise and edit raw notes and photos.

## Helpful, not blocking the next review

- Brand guide or preferred colours/fonts.
- Current school calendar highlights for the next 60–90 days.
- Testimonials only where the speaker and publication permission are documented.
- Policies, accreditation or awards only with current source documents.
- Class size, teacher qualifications, languages and meals only where the school wants these comparison facts published.
