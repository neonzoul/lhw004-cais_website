# CAIS v0.3 — research and hypotheses

Research date: 5 November 2026. This document separates observed evidence from ideas that still need validation.

## Decision context

Parents rarely choose a school from a slogan alone. The website must first answer the comparison questions — school type, ages, curriculum, location, daily experience and next step — then show credible evidence of how CAIS feels and teaches.

The website has two jobs:

1. Help a family decide whether CAIS deserves a visit or conversation.
2. Publish clear, attributable school information that Search and AI systems can retrieve, compare and cite.

Google states that the same SEO fundamentals apply to its AI search features; useful, original, people-first content remains the priority. There is no separate technical “GEO switch”. Entity clarity, accessible pages, internal links, accurate structured data and first-hand content make the school easier to understand across both search and AI-assisted journeys.

## Parent decision cohorts

| Cohort | Likely question | Website response in v0.3 |
|---|---|---|
| Relocating international family | “Will this school fit my child before we arrive in Phuket?” | English-first essentials, online-conversation option, location context and admissions checklist |
| Local expat comparison shopper | “How is this different from the schools already on my list?” | five-second facts, values translated into classroom practice, real media and stage map |
| Values-led parent | “Will my child be known, calm and happy — without losing academic foundations?” | relationships, mindfulness, outdoor learning and academic practice shown together |
| Thai/international family | “Can my child gain an international foundation while staying connected to place and culture?” | British EYFS/KS1 alongside Thai culture, Phuket experiences and community |
| Search/AI-first parent | “Give me a shortlist of schools matching my criteria.” | explicit facts, focused pages, descriptive headings, School schema and evidence-led articles |

These are working cohorts, not measured audience segments. Analytics and enquiry questions should validate or revise them after launch.

## Competitor pattern audit

| Website | Useful pattern observed | Opportunity for CAIS |
|---|---|---|
| Bamboo Valley Phuket | ages/program, visit, enquiry, WhatsApp and video-call options are easy to find | match decision clarity; differentiate through the CAIS learning philosophy and evidence |
| Lighthouse International Kindergarten | classes, parent information, food, calendar, gallery, fees and tour action | build a complete parent information layer once approved data is available |
| Little Lions Phuket | age range, hours, curriculum, languages and director profile are explicit | make practical facts visible early, then add credible educator profiles |
| International School of Phuket | reasons to choose, curriculum, teachers, class size, fees, food and schedule | turn each CAIS promise into observable practice and publish missing logistics after approval |
| Larger Phuket international schools | deep admissions and curriculum architecture | CAIS can be simpler, warmer and more personal while remaining complete |

The opportunity is not to look larger than CAIS is. It is to make a close-knit early-years school easier to understand and trust than alternatives with more pages but less coherent storytelling.

## Positioning hypothesis

> A close-knit international learning community in Thalang, where happiness, relationships, nature and Thai culture help young children grow with confidence.

The public-facing homepage uses a shorter factual expression:

> A close-knit international school where children build strong academic foundations through caring relationships, purposeful play, outdoor learning and mindful moments.

### Why this is credible enough to test

- CAIS publicly describes a British curriculum for ages 2–7 and names Academic Excellence, Outdoor & Active Learning, and Mindfulness & Well-being as pillars.
- Supplied media shows warm adult–child interaction, making, group moments and outdoor experiences.
- Public school activity posts include Thai culture/community experiences such as a Phuket Peranakan Museum visit and Thai dessert activity.
- “Happiness is the beginning of learning” already exists in the approved direction from the partner team.

### What would invalidate or change it

- School leadership does not want Thai culture or mindfulness to be part of the market position.
- Families primarily choose CAIS for a different factor such as location, price, language or a specific educator.
- School practice cannot supply current evidence for the promises.

## Experience hypotheses applied

### H1 — clarity before poetry

If a parent can identify school type, age range, curriculum, location and next action in five seconds, more qualified families will continue exploring.

Applied: factual eyebrow, decision strip, persistent visit CTA and descriptive page titles.

Measure: hero-to-Curriculum/Admissions/Contact click-through; qualified enquiry rate.

### H2 — value → practice → parent meaning

If each value is paired with what a child does and why it matters, broad claims such as “holistic” or “nature-connected” become more credible.

Applied: three pillars, day flow, gallery captions, curriculum model and first article.

Measure: depth of visit, article engagement, parent questions mentioning a specific practice.

### H3 — help parents picture their child here

If families see real activity, educator interaction and environment before visiting, the website reduces uncertainty and improves visit quality.

Applied: supplied images, learning-led captions, classroom-video direction and school-life gallery.

Measure: gallery/video engagement and visit conversion.

### H4 — one journey for local and relocating families

If contact and admissions support school visits and online conversations, CAIS can capture suitable families before they arrive in Phuket.

Applied: relocating-family guidance, location field, preferred-start field and online-conversation option.

Measure: source location, enquiry type, scheduled conversations and eventual enrolment.

### H5 — owned knowledge compounds trust

If CAIS regularly publishes accurate, attributable explanations of its educational practice, parents and AI systems have a primary source to rely on rather than reconstructing the school from social posts and directories.

Applied: Articles hub, evidence-led first article, School/Article structured data and focused real URLs.

Measure: non-brand search impressions, AI/Search referral patterns where available, branded search growth and assisted enquiries. Do not treat AI citations as guaranteed.

### H6 — currentness belongs after understanding

If the first current event or announcement appears only after the parent understands the school and sees real learning, it can prove that the community is active without interrupting the initial fit decision.

Applied: one-story-at-a-time Events & Announcements slider after the silent-video slot and before family-fit pathways.

Measure: event interaction, repeat visits and event-to-enquiry actions. The school must replace prototype slides with dated, approved updates and remove expired information.
